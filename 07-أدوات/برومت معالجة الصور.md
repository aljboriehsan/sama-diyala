# برومت معالجة وتوليد صور الوجهات

يُنسخ إلى نموذج تعديل أو توليد صور. يُملأ ما بين الأقواس [ ] في كل مرة، ويُحذف ما لا يلزم.

```
You are editing (or, only if asked, generating) a destination photo for a travel agency's social media offer ad. The photo is the full background of the ad. Text and logo are added later by me, so never add any text, logo, watermark, frame or border.

TASK
- Destination: [e.g. Istanbul — Bosphorus / Beirut — Raouche Rock]
- Occasion / season: [e.g. New Year / winter / summer / spring / none]
- What to do: [e.g. light grade only / make it blue hour / add light snow on the rocks / add one strip of fireworks / remove the fan / straighten the horizon]

REALISM (most important)
- Must look like a real, unedited photograph taken by a good photographer. No "AI look": no plastic textures, no oversaturated colours, no HDR halos, no glowing edges, no impossible light.
- Keep the real landmark exactly as it is: correct shape, correct surroundings, correct scale. Do not invent buildings, islands, mountains or monuments that are not there.
- Prefer the smallest edit that achieves the goal. Do not change the time of day or the weather unless I ask for it.
- Any added element (snow, clouds, fireworks, lights) must follow the scene's light and perspective: snow settles on upward-facing surfaces and ledges, is bluish in shade and never sits on vertical faces or on the water; clouds shrink and flatten toward the horizon; fireworks are in the sky, behind the subject, and light the sky around them faintly.
- Keep the original grain and sharpness; added parts must match them.

COMPOSITION (the ad layout depends on this)
- Final ratio 4:5 (1080 x 1350). Also keep it usable as 9:16 (1080 x 1920) when possible.
- Top 35% of the frame: calm sky or soft background with no important detail (logo, destination name and price go there). Nothing important behind the centre of this area.
- The main subject (landmark) starts below ~37% of the height and sits in the middle band.
- Bottom ~12%: calm, darker area (water, ground, shadow) for small footer text on the left and right.
- Horizon perfectly level. Verticals of buildings straight.
- Leave the subject whole: do not crop the top of the landmark.

COLOUR AND LIGHT
- Natural, clean and slightly cinematic, suitable for an official company (agent of a national airline). Calm and elegant, not flashy.
- Gentle contrast, true whites, deep but not crushed shadows, moderate saturation.
- Warm scenes: soft golden light. Night scenes: real blue hour, deep blue sky with warm city lights. Winter: cooler white balance, slightly desaturated, overcast or soft light.
- The ad uses white or dark-green text with gold for the price, so the top area should be either clearly dark (for white text) or clearly light (for green text), not busy mid-tones.

PEOPLE AND CONTENT
- People only small, natural and in the background, or seen from behind; no close-up identifiable faces. Modest clothing suitable for a conservative Iraqi family audience.
- No prominent religious symbols that are not part of the landmark itself. No alcohol, no swimwear close-ups.
- No brand logos, shop signs or readable text in the scene if they can be avoided.
- Seasonal elements only when asked, and subtle (e.g. one strip of fireworks for New Year, light snow for winter).

OUTPUT
- At least 2000 px on the long side, high quality JPEG.
- Give me the edited photo only, and a one-line note of what you changed.
```

## ملاحظات
- البرومت بالإنجليزية لأن أغلب نماذج الصور تفهمها أدق.
- القسم الأهم "COMPOSITION"، لأن قالب الإعلان ثابت: الترويسة في أعلى 35%، والمدة والرقم في أسفل 12%.
- في الصور المولّدة بالكامل: تأكد أن المعلم مطابق للواقع، وتذكّر قاعدة عدم نشر صور مولّدة توحي بأنها حقيقية.
